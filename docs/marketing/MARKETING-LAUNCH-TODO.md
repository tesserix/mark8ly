## Operational notes

- **Order matters:** profile polish → narrative posts → warm-up → DMs.
  Skipping any earlier step burns the later ones.
- **Don't DM from `@mark8ly` ever.** Brand accounts get filtered.
- **Don't rename `@mahesh.sangawar` → `@mark8ly`.** Burns 14d cooldown,
  algo de-rank, broken old links, loses founder-voice asset.
- **Authentic photos > stock or AI imagery** for posts #1–6. Editorial
  brands win on authenticity; an imperfect real photo beats a perfect
  rendered one.
- **Two posts back-to-back dilutes both.** 36–48h gap minimum between
  major narrative posts.
- **Reply rate target:** 10%+ from scraped cold list with personalized
  opener. <5% means the opener or profile needs work; iterate before
  scaling volume.
