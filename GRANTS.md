# mark8ly — Grants, Funding & Startup Programs (Australia)

## Tier 1 — High Priority

### R&D Tax Incentive (RDTI)
- **Amount:** 43.5% refundable tax offset on eligible R&D expenditure (turnover < $20M)
- **What qualifies:** AI-assisted development, multi-tenant SaaS architecture, payment provider abstraction layer, multi-country tax/shipping integration, React Native mobile app build pipeline
- **Eligible costs:** Developer salaries/contractor fees, cloud infrastructure (GCP), software tools, R&D-related travel
- **How to apply:** Lodge via ATO with an R&D Tax Incentive registration through Industry Innovation and Science Australia (IISA). Register activities before end of financial year, claim in tax return.
- **Tip:** Engage an R&D tax consultant — they work on a % of recovered amount (typically 10-15%). They know how to frame software dev as R&D.
- **Link:** https://business.gov.au/grants-and-programs/research-and-development-tax-incentive
- **Status:** [ ] Not started

### Export Market Development Grant (EMDG)
- **Amount:** Up to $150K over 3 grant years (50% reimbursement of eligible export expenses)
- **What qualifies:** mark8ly targets India, SEA, US, EU markets from Australia
- **Eligible costs:** Overseas marketing, localization, trade shows, market research, IP protection, overseas hosting costs, travel for market development
- **How to apply:** Through Austrade. Must have spent at least $15K on eligible export promotion activities.
- **Tip:** Start tracking all India/SEA-related costs now — hosting in asia-south1, Razorpay/Delhivery integration work, market research.
- **Link:** https://www.austrade.gov.au/en/how-we-can-help-you/programs-and-grants/emdg
- **Status:** [ ] Not started

### Entrepreneurs' Programme — Accelerating Commercialisation
- **Amount:** Up to $1M matched funding (dollar-for-dollar)
- **What qualifies:** Novel product commercialisation. mark8ly's 0% transaction fee model + multi-country commerce platform targeting underserved emerging markets.
- **How to apply:** Via business.gov.au. Requires business plan, market validation, and commercial potential evidence.
- **Link:** https://business.gov.au/grants-and-programs/accelerating-commercialisation
- **Status:** [ ] Not started
