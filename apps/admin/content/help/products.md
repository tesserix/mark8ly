## Creating Products

Navigate to **Products** in the sidebar and click **Add product** to create a new listing. Every product needs a title and at least one variant with a price. The title should be clear and descriptive since it appears in search results, social shares, and your storefront catalog.

Write product descriptions that help customers make purchasing decisions. Focus on materials, dimensions, use cases, and what makes your product different. Avoid generic filler text. A well-written description reduces returns and support questions.

## Variants and Options

Products can have multiple variants representing different configurations like size, color, or material. Define your options first, such as "Size" with values "S, M, L, XL," and Mark8ly generates the corresponding variant matrix.

Each variant has its own SKU, price, compare-at price, cost price, inventory quantity, and weight. This granularity lets you track inventory and profitability at the variant level. Use meaningful SKU conventions like "TEE-BLK-M" so your team can identify items at a glance during packing.

## Product Media

Upload high-quality images for each product. Mark8ly accepts JPEG, PNG, and WebP formats. The first image becomes the primary display image on your storefront. You can reorder images by dragging them into your preferred sequence.

Consider uploading images from multiple angles: front, back, detail shots, and lifestyle context. Customers who can see a product clearly from different perspectives convert at higher rates. Use the image cropping tool to ensure consistent aspect ratios across your catalog.

## Categories and Organization

Organize your products into categories to help customers browse your store. Categories appear in your storefront navigation and filter options. Each product can belong to one primary category and additional secondary categories.

Keep your category structure shallow, ideally two levels deep at most. Deeply nested categories confuse customers and make navigation harder. If you have a large catalog, use tags for flexible cross-cutting organization in addition to categories.

## Product Status and Publishing

Products have three statuses: **Draft**, **Active**, and **Archived**. Drafts are only visible in your admin. Active products appear on your storefront and in search. Archived products are hidden from customers but preserved in your admin for reference.

Use draft status while building out product details. Switch to active when the listing is complete with images, descriptions, accurate pricing, and sufficient inventory. Archive products that are permanently discontinued rather than deleting them, since archived products preserve order history references.

## Bulk Operations

For stores with many products, Mark8ly supports bulk actions. Select multiple products from the product list and apply actions like changing status, updating categories, or adjusting pricing. Bulk operations save significant time when managing seasonal catalogs or running promotions.

You can also export your product catalog as a CSV for offline editing or import purposes. This is particularly useful when migrating from another platform or making sweeping price adjustments.
