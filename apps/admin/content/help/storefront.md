Your storefront is the public face of your brand. Mark8ly ships a default editorial theme that looks refined on any device, and **Settings → Storefront** gives you full control over how it presents your brand.

## Theme and layout

The theme editor lets you adjust:

- Primary color
- Background tone
- Heading font
- Layout structure (header, hero, product grid)

Changes preview in real time, so you can experiment freely. When you're satisfied, save your changes — they go live immediately.

## Navigation and pages

Your storefront navigation is generated automatically from your product categories and any custom pages you create. To reorder navigation items or add external links, use the navigation editor in the storefront settings.

Custom pages are useful for:

- About Us
- Contact
- FAQ
- Policy pages (privacy, refunds, shipping, terms)

Each page supports rich text formatting, images, and embedded content.

> Keep your page count focused. Customers prefer fewer, well-organized pages over a sprawling sitemap.

## Branding and identity

Upload your logo and favicon under the storefront branding section.

- The **logo** appears in the header and on transactional emails.
- The **favicon** shows in browser tabs and bookmarks.

Use an SVG or high-resolution PNG for the sharpest rendering across devices.

Mark8ly automatically generates Open Graph images for social sharing based on your branding. When someone shares a link to your store, the preview card includes your store name, logo, and a clean layout that represents your brand.

## Mobile experience

All Mark8ly storefronts are responsive by default. The layout adapts to phone, tablet, and desktop screens without any additional configuration.

- Navigation collapses into a mobile menu.
- Product grids reflow to single columns.
- Touch targets are sized for comfortable tapping.

Always test on multiple devices during setup. Open your store URL on your phone, watch image loading speed on mobile networks, and confirm checkout works smoothly on smaller screens.

## SEO basics

Each product and page has dedicated fields for SEO title and meta description. Fill these with natural, keyword-rich text that accurately describes the content.

Mark8ly handles the technical side automatically:

- Clean URLs from product handles and page slugs
- Structured data for rich search results
- Auto-generated and submitted sitemap

For the best results:

- Keep product titles descriptive.
- Write a unique description for every product (no duplicates).
- Use alt text on every image.
