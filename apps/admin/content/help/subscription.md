Mark8ly subscriptions are managed under **Settings → Subscription**. From there you can view your current plan, update your payment method, download invoices, and switch tiers.

## Plans and billing

Each plan includes a set of features, transaction limits, and a support level. Subscriptions are billed **monthly** through Stripe.

- **Upgrade** at any time — changes are prorated and you get immediate access to the new features.
- **Downgrade** at any time — changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

## Managing your subscription

The subscription page surfaces:

- Current plan and renewal date
- Payment method on file
- Invoice history (downloadable as PDF for your records)

Update your payment method whenever your card changes or expires.

> If a payment fails, Mark8ly retries the charge several times over a few days. If all retries fail, your store enters a **grace period** where it stays accessible but you can't make changes. Update your payment method to restore full access.

## What higher tiers unlock

Higher-tier plans typically include:

- Custom domains
- Increased product and order limits
- Advanced analytics
- Priority support
- Lower transaction fees

Review the plan comparison on the subscription page to see exactly what each tier offers. As your store grows, upgrading is usually a smaller cost than the time you'll save with better tooling.

> Consider upgrading when you're consistently approaching your current plan's limits, or when you need a specific feature like advanced marketing or larger team management.

## Cancellation

You can cancel at any time from the subscription settings page. Here's what happens:

1. Your store remains active until the end of the current billing period.
2. After that, the store enters **read-only mode** — customers can't place orders, but your data is preserved.
3. Reactivating restores full access immediately.

> Data is retained for **90 days** after cancellation. After that it may be permanently deleted. If you want to keep an export of your orders or customer list, do it before the 90-day window closes.

## Invoice and tax information

Add your **business tax ID** and **billing address** to your subscription settings so they appear on your invoices. This matters for:

- Claiming business-expense deductions
- VAT reverse-charge in EU jurisdictions
- Maintaining accurate financial records

Mark8ly issues invoices that comply with standard invoicing requirements, including VAT identification where applicable. Contact support if you need a custom invoice format or additional billing documentation.
