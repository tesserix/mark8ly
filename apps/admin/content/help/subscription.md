## Subscription Plans

Mark8ly offers tiered subscription plans to match your business size and needs. View your current plan and available upgrades under **Settings > Subscription**. Each plan includes a set of features, transaction limits, and support levels.

Your subscription is billed monthly through Stripe. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time, and changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle. Upgrades provide immediate access to new features while your billing adjusts proportionally.

## Managing Your Subscription

The subscription page shows your current plan, billing date, payment method on file, and invoice history. Download past invoices for your accounting records. Update your payment method if your card changes or expires.

If your payment fails, Mark8ly retries the charge several times over a few days. If all retries fail, your store enters a grace period where it remains accessible but you cannot make changes. Update your payment method to restore full access.

## Plan Features

Higher-tier plans unlock additional capabilities like custom domains, increased product limits, advanced analytics, priority support, and lower transaction fees. Review the plan comparison on the subscription page to understand what each tier offers.

As your store grows, upgrading to a higher plan is an investment in tools that help you sell more efficiently. Consider upgrading when you are consistently approaching your current plan's limits or when you need features like advanced marketing tools or team management.

## Cancellation

You can cancel your subscription at any time from the subscription settings page. Your store remains active until the end of your current billing period. After cancellation, your store enters read-only mode: customers cannot place orders, but your data is preserved.

If you change your mind, you can reactivate your subscription at any time and regain full access to your store. Data is retained for ninety days after cancellation, after which it may be permanently deleted.

## Invoice and Tax Information

Add your business tax ID and billing address to your subscription settings so they appear on your invoices. This information is important for claiming business expense deductions and maintaining accurate financial records.

Mark8ly issues invoices that comply with standard invoicing requirements including VAT identification where applicable. Contact support if you need a custom invoice format or additional billing documentation.
