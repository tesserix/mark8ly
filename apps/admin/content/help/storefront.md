## Theme and Layout

Your storefront is the public face of your brand. Mark8ly provides a default editorial theme that looks refined on any device, but you have full control over its appearance through **Settings > Storefront**.

The theme editor lets you adjust your primary color, background tone, heading font, and layout structure. Changes preview in real time so you can experiment without publishing. When you are satisfied, save your changes and they go live immediately.

## Navigation and Pages

Your storefront navigation is generated automatically from your product categories and any custom pages you create. To reorder navigation items or add external links, use the navigation editor in the storefront settings.

Custom pages are useful for About Us, Contact, FAQ, and policy pages. Each page supports rich text formatting, images, and embedded content. Keep your page count focused: customers prefer fewer, well-organized pages over a sprawling sitemap.

## Branding and Identity

Upload your logo and favicon under the storefront branding section. Your logo appears in the header and on transactional emails. The favicon shows in browser tabs and bookmarks. Use an SVG or high-resolution PNG for the sharpest rendering across devices.

Mark8ly automatically generates Open Graph images for social sharing based on your branding. When someone shares a link to your store on social media, the preview card includes your store name, logo, and a clean layout that represents your brand.

## Mobile Experience

All Mark8ly storefronts are responsive by default. The layout adapts to phone, tablet, and desktop screens without any additional configuration. Navigation collapses into a mobile menu, product grids reflow to single columns, and touch targets are sized for comfortable tapping.

Test your storefront on multiple devices during setup. Use your browser's responsive design mode or simply open your store URL on your phone. Pay attention to image loading speed on mobile networks and ensure that your checkout flow works smoothly on smaller screens.

## SEO Basics

Each product and page has fields for SEO title and meta description. Fill these in with natural, keyword-rich text that accurately describes the content. Mark8ly generates clean URLs from your product handles and page slugs, and includes structured data for rich search results.

Your sitemap is generated automatically and submitted to search engines. For best results, keep your product titles descriptive, write unique descriptions for each product, and use alt text on every image.
