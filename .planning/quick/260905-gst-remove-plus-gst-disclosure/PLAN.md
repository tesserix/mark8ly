# Remove the "Plus 10% GST" disclosure

Tesserix is **not registered for GST**, so it cannot charge GST. The prices
charged are already correct (A$29.00, no tax added). The defect is that three
surfaces tell Australian visitors "Plus 10% GST for Australian businesses." —
a charge that does not happen and cannot lawfully happen while unregistered.

Decision recorded on #699. This reverses mark8ly#600, which added the
disclosure on the reasoning that omitting it understated the price. That was
right when GST was assumed to apply; with no registration it is inverted —
the disclosure now OVERSTATES what the buyer pays and promises a tax invoice
we cannot issue.

## Deliberately NOT in scope

- **`apps/onboarding/app/terms/page.tsx:104`** — published Terms of Service
  ("plan prices are stated exclusive of GST"). Legal copy; a human decides.
- **`tax_behavior: "exclusive"` in `catalog_data.go`** — MUST NOT CHANGE.
  GST registration is expected later. Keeping the prices tax-exclusive means
  enabling `automatic_tax` then adds 10% on top of the same Price objects
  (A$29.00 -> A$31.90) with no repricing and no migrating subscribers. Marking
  them inclusive would force exactly that migration. Leave them alone.
- Anything under `internal/tax/`, `billing/tax/`, storefront invoices or
  `TaxTab.tsx` — that is the MERCHANT's own product tax, a different domain.
